Company Hub by Rococo Creative

Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Rococo Creative LLC ("Rococo", "we", "us") operates Company Hub, a web application for its client companies. This policy explains what information Company Hub holds, where it comes from, how it is used and the choices available. It applies to everyone who signs in to Company Hub and to information read from tools a company connects.

Who is responsible

Company Hub is provided to businesses, not to the public. Each client company (the "Company") decides who may sign in and what information goes into its hub. Rococo processes that information on the Company's behalf to provide the service.

Information we hold

  • Account details: a person's name, work email address, role and job title, added by their Company's administrators.
  • Information the Company enters: such as goals, reviews and ratings, key figures, compensation, procedures, resources, projects and files.
  • Sign-in and activity records: when someone signs in, and a log of changes made in the hub, kept so the Company can see who changed what.
  • Information from connected tools: described in the next section.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell any information.

Connected tools, including QuickBooks

A Company's owner or administrator may connect outside tools, approving access on that tool's own sign-in page. Company Hub only ever reads from them:

  • QuickBooks Online (Intuit): accounting records such as invoices and payments, and the name of the connected company file.
  • Google: the connecting account's email address and the spreadsheets the Company chooses, read only.
  • GoHighLevel: opportunities, users and location details, read only.

This information is used only to show reporting to that Company's own authorized people inside Company Hub. It is never shared with another company, sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models. Company Hub does not create, change or delete anything in a connected tool.

The access a tool grants is stored on our servers in a place only Company Hub's server code can read. It is never sent to a web browser. When a Company disconnects a tool, Company Hub deletes that access. A Company can also revoke access at any time from the tool's own settings, for example the Apps or Connected apps page in QuickBooks Online.

How we use information

  • to provide Company Hub and show each Company its own information;
  • to sign people in and send the emails the service needs, such as sign-in links, invitations and task notices;
  • to keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems;
  • to meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run Company Hub. They process information only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and server functions, hosted in the United States.
  • Vercel: hosting for the web application.
  • Resend: delivery of service emails.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of Rococo, our clients or others. If Rococo's business is transferred, information may move with it under the same protections.

How we protect it

Information travels over encrypted connections. Each Company's data is walled off from every other Company's in the database itself, and people see only what their role allows. Access to connected tools is held server side only.

How long we keep it

We keep a Company's information while it is a Rococo client. When a Company asks, or after its service ends, we delete or return its information unless the law requires us to keep it. An administrator can delete a person from their Company at any time, which deletes that person's history and sign-in.

Your choices

If you want to see, correct or delete information about you, ask your Company's administrator, who manages your account, or contact us below and we will work with your Company to respond. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law.

Children

Company Hub is for people at work and is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date above, and for material changes we will tell each Company's administrators.
